1st-year B.Tech Computer Science student at Manipal Institute of Technology, Bangalore — with a 9.85 CGPA and a hands-on background in game development and artificial intelligence. From shipping a 2D strategy game under a 24-hour deadline at TechSolstice to engineering a real-time face recognition system with OpenCV, I'm driven by the challenge of turning complex logic into functional, interactive reality.
